Privacy

The design tools on this site

The design tools run in your own browser. The sizes and options you choose, and the design files you download, are made on your computer. They are not uploaded to this site, and this site has no accounts, no sign-in and no forms.

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and to count how many times each tool's download button is used. It records which tool, not what you designed. Google Analytics sets its own cookies to do this.

The pages also load a few code libraries from public networks (unpkg and the Tailwind CSS network) and video thumbnail pictures from YouTube. Those services can see that your browser asked for the file, as with any web page.

Polyphony Home

Polyphony Home is a separate, private tool. It is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else is asked to sign in to it.

With the owner's permission, it reads two things that belong to the owner: the owner's own Google Drive, to keep a backup copy on the owner's own computer, and the comments on the owner's own YouTube channel, so they can be read and answered.

The copies stay on the owner's own computer and home storage. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to anyone else, and it collects no information about any other person. The owner can remove its access at any time in their Google Account, under Security, "Your connections to third-party apps and services".
